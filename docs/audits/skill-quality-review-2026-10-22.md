# Education Agent Skills — practical-quality review and refinement recommendation

22 October 2026. Repository `GarethManning/education-agent-skills`; baseline main `6bbbce418f82e11044009c9f3b7373a354de5bd0`. Independent educational open-source project. **Review and proposals only: no skill, frontmatter, registry, bundle, workflow, installed plugin or remote changed.** Earlier standards-audit documents are retained separately.

## The recommendation

**Strengthen the library through targeted corrections and selective reorganization. Preserve its educational substance, identities and architecture.** The existing inclusion/exclusion rules are valuable. Their presence does not ensure that every instruction, worked example, handoff and generated claim implements those rules consistently.

Applying a proposed eight-criterion practical-quality rubric produces these action queues:

| Primary action | Skills | Interpretation |
|---|---:|---|
| Keep | 108 | No sufficiently justified content change identified in this bounded review; some have optional metadata/input-convention refinements |
| Reorganize | 20 | Preserve approach; consider a lighter agent entry point and referenced examples/background |
| Revise | 37 | A targeted content, workflow, evidence-labeling or boundary improvement is supported by specific passages |
| Remove | 0 | No evidence justifies deleting a skill or abandoning its educational approach |

These are **editorial/workflow decisions, not pass rates, scores of educational efficacy or37 unsafe skills**. Several revisions are small. Seven receive higher priority because they concern safeguarding/support, manufactured learner evidence or unbounded interaction gates; that is an assessment of the instructions, not evidence of actual harm. Original-framework status is not a reason for downgrade. Evidence-label-only or suggested-chain metadata issues do not by themselves put a skill in the substantive-revision queue.

**Coverage matters:** all165 files received structured source screening and1320 criterion entries. An independent inspector fully read28 skills in student-learning, inclusive-design and wellbeing-motivation-agency; the primary auditor reviewed structured dossiers for the other137 and performed focused deeper reads of flagged passages across domains. A second independent challenge checked contested judgments. This is not a claim that every line of all137 other files received equal-depth expert review. Counts prioritize work; they must not be used to infer domain failure rates from unequal review depth. Actual model adherence, comparative usefulness, classroom feasibility and learner benefit were not measured.

## What the criteria add to your existing rules

The companion [rubric](skill-quality-rubric-2026-10-22.md) retains inclusion/exclusion policy and asks:

1. Is the job and intended user clear enough to select the right skill?
2. Does it add specific educational expertise or useful decisions?
3. Do inputs, workflow, examples and handoffs agree?
4. Are educational/evidence claims proportionate and honestly labelled?
5. Does it adapt to relevant context while preserving teacher/learner agency?
6. Is the promised output usable with proportionate workload?
7. Are important boundaries and failure routes present where decisions occur?
8. Is the information organized economically without losing needed substance?

A single mean score would hide consequential problems. A sophisticated, research-linked workflow can still contain one contradictory branch or an inappropriate safeguard. Conversely, a lengthy document with useful worked examples is not bad simply because it is long. Each recommendation has source evidence, a preserved-remainder statement and the smallest useful change. No pilot or learner experiment is a prerequisite for this review.

## What should change first

### 1. Correct contradictions and example accuracy

These are the clearest improvements because they do not depend on proving a new pedagogy.

- `skills/student-learning/fading-manager/SKILL.md:111–117,195–201`: progression requires three sessions with hints0–1, but the example advances after two with hints1–2. Its retrieval label also differs from its paired producer. Make the rule and example agree; keep the explicitly heuristic nature of the thresholds.
- `skills/memory-learning-science/retrieval-practice-generator/SKILL.md:109–115,150`: novice recognition questions are allowed, then prohibited by an all-questions rule/self-check. State the intended exception consistently.
- `skills/curriculum-alignment/developmental-band-translator/SKILL.md:126,148–152`: initial no-age halt conflicts with override/flat-framework logic. Clarify precedence, keep uncertainty and human review.
- `skills/curriculum-alignment/kud-chart-author/SKILL.md:129,141`: the suggested band translator needs already-decomposed input; it is not a raw-document decomposition service.
- `skills/student-learning/stuck-and-error-diagnosis-coach/SKILL.md:184,188,224`: numerical example shifts2.5 to2, and its general claim that kinematics needs time is false. Under constant acceleration, `v² = u² + 2aΔx` does not require elapsed time. Correct the example while retaining diagnosis-based help.
- `skills/student-learning/transfer-bridge/SKILL.md:177–236`: the worked example tests equal-share allocation arithmetic while claiming transfer of market-price formation. Use the same target mechanism, or state analogy limits and do not count it as demonstrated transfer.

### 2. Make challenge productive without trapping the learner

Retain retrieval, self-explanation, graduated hints and productive struggle. The concern is not that these methods ask learners to think.

- `productive-failure-protocol:84,110,129–142` can require distinct attempts indefinitely before consolidation. Add a finite, adjustable exploration/recovery route. Analyzing a flawed alternative after a correct solution can remain: it is legitimate contrasting-case teaching, not intrinsically a defect.
- `progressive-hint-ladder:149` treats frustration as evidence that difficulty is correct. Frustration alone cannot establish that; check understanding, access and desired next support. Six levels and a no-full-answer design remain legitimate choices within its remit.
- `retrieve-first-gate` and `explain-first-interrogator` need an accessible pause/foundation-instruction route for missing prior knowledge rather than an indefinite request for another attempt. Preserve the attempt-based feedback method.
- `unassisted-evidence-checkpoint` can retain honest unassisted tagging while allowing stop/defer/assisted-resume; stopping does not require falsely passing a checkpoint.

### 3. Preserve observations and uncertainty instead of manufacturing evidence

- `confidence-calibration-check:135–139` directs an unseen-topic learner's confidence to be set to5. Keep unknown/not-captured or the learner's own report rather than inventing a number; feedback should describe this task, not diagnose a stable personal trait from one response.
- `ai-claim-checker:62,90–92,102,139,153,157–161` teaches identifying potential uncertainty and proposing a verification method. Keep that job. Permit justified agreement and distinguish a checking plan from verification actually performed. Do not label agreement alone low epistemic vigilance or make source consultation compulsory in every use.
- `weekly-agency-review` should distinguish recorded evidence, recalled estimates and interpretation; do not infer causal consolidation/dependence from incomparable tasks.
- `wellbeing-learning-connection-mapper:184,203–215,225,249–255` includes unsupported/overgeneralized impact claims and a local trend not supplied in its fictional scenario. Label assumptions/example data explicitly and allow the conclusion that evidence is insufficient. This is a problem in the worked example, not proof that live school data was fabricated.

### 4. Put support and safeguarding exceptions before the decision

- `original-frameworks/inclusive-design-orchestrator:124–128,202,239,248` makes individualized support conditional on earlier tiers. Keep universal-first as a planning priority, while preserving established/urgent access support in parallel. Do not infer one jurisdiction's legal rules.
- `trauma-informed-practice-designer:215,225–227,250–265,286–293` needs clear separation between observable behavior and trauma hypothesis, immediate safety/local plans and routine support referral. Tears do not confirm a trauma mechanism. Preserve calm response, predictability and dignity; have consequential response guidance reviewed by an appropriate specialist rather than replacing it with another universal script.
- `restorative-practice-protocol-designer:192–224,301` already recognizes that persistent bullying/power asymmetry can make a conference unsuitable, but chooses face-to-face conferencing for persistent accent mockery. Apply that existing suitability/consent caution before selecting a meeting. Do not claim the skill has no consent protections at all.
- `ruler-emotional-literacy-sequence:194–206,227` needs a consistent private/fictional/pass route for emotion and causal disclosure. Unnamed public notes are not necessarily anonymous in a room where placements are observed.

Avoid adding a generic safety essay to every skill. Place short, relevant boundaries at the consequential decision; store supporting background separately. The two disputed UDL skills are retained: brief group/task context does not itself imply collection/storage of named plans. A shared optional functional-needs/minimization convention is proportionate.

### 5. Calibrate evidence wording without relitigating inclusion

The earlier audit independently checked sampled claims: spacing's Cepeda study count and claimed exact schedule optimum; explain-first's durability/transfer claim; CAST2018 described as current; a Woloshyn bibliography error. Correct those narrow points. A framework's evidence label does not prove this exact generated script or threshold, but absence of an exact-script trial does not make an adaptation invalid.

For original/composite methods, preserve useful distinctive rules and clear practitioner/synthesis labels. Do not change all frameworks to generic advice or remove their research. Review unsupported words such as “confirms,” “guarantees,” “validates,” “always” and “never” in context; a keyword alone is not grounds to remove a statement. For example, `professional-learning/competency-framework-translator:116,128–130` should keep model interpretation separate from verified Original Framework Wording.

### 6. Make the actual delivered skill carry its essential cautions

The repository has two materially different reading surfaces. Local clients can read the full skill; MCP extracts only Prompt/System Prompt. Some valuable caveats exist solely in Known Limitations outside that extraction. Examples verified by exact source-versus-bundle comparison:

- `retrieval-practice-generator:221`: answers/terminology should be checked against the taught source; absent from extracted prompt.
- `ladder-of-inference-reflection:173–175`: recent significant harm and power asymmetry limit appropriate use; generic dignity rules are already in core but the specific boundary is absent.
- `historical-document-set-curator:290`: it proposes sources but cannot supply them; the active output asks for specific identities without clear verified/proposed status.

This is not a request to inline every research paragraph. Preserve the smallest decision-relevant caution in the core. Optional detailed resources must be available to whichever client needs them; a relative path in an inaccessible hosted environment is not a complete solution. Avoid an infrastructure redesign where a concise core correction suffices.

For reorganization, preserve inputs, decision sequence, critical exceptions, output contract and one compact worked example in the core. Extended transcripts, alternative templates and research exposition can become clearly named, conditionally loaded references. Correct contradictory examples before moving them. Keep a readable human guide. Never cut a rule merely because it is long; ask what error it prevents and where it is needed.

## Recommended execution, if subsequent changes are authorized

1. Preserve a tagged/frozen accepted baseline in Git; do not duplicate active old/new/v2 skill directories.
2. Correct high-confidence factual and rule/example contradictions as small edits.
3. Correct consequential support, safeguarding and learner-evidence boundaries with appropriate human expertise.
4. Improve context/agency recovery and make core warnings survive MCP extraction.
5. Reorganize selected long entry points; do not bulk-shorten165 prompts or force every skill into one template.
6. Preserve IDs/schemas/citations; regenerate registry and bundle for accepted source changes; check source/core parity and existing tests. Keep security/infrastructure repairs separate from content edits.
7. Retain reasons and concrete before/after examples with each accepted change. A task-output comparison can resolve a disputed choice later, but this review does not impose a pilot or learner study before fixing clear defects.

The full per-skill proposal is in [the matrix](skill-quality-review-matrix.csv), with all eight judgments and source links. [Structured evidence](skill-quality-review-evidence.json) also specifies what to preserve, mandatory core instructions, optional resource candidates, source hashes and review depth. The working rubric and queue are proposals, not a recorded founder decision or an implementation authorization.
